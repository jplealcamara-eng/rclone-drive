Privacy policy

rclone-drive is a personal OAuth client. It is used only by its owner, to access the owner's own Google Drive with rclone on the owner's own computer.

  • No data is collected, stored or shared by this app or its owner beyond the owner's own files.
  • Google Drive data is transferred only between Google and the owner's computer.
  • Nothing is sent to any third party, and nothing is used for advertising or analytics.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
